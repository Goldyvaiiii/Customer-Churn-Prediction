# Enterprise Customer Retention Playbook

This playbook outlines standardized workflows and strategies for customer retention based on risk categories, contract types, and customer usage patterns.

## 1. Retention Action Plan by Churn Risk Category

### High Risk (Churn Probability > 70%)
*   **Time to Action**: Within 24-48 hours.
*   **Action Owner**: Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) or Account Executive.
*   **Retention Strategies**:
    *   **Proactive Executive Outreach**: Schedule an urgent review call with key decision-makers.
    *   **Financial Incentives**: Offer a contract renewal discount (up to 20%) or a free subscription month in exchange for a contract extension.
    *   **Friction Removal**: If the user is facing technical problems, assign a senior support engineer to resolve outstanding issues immediately.
    *   **Down-sell Options**: If the issue is budget-related, propose a lower tier that retains core features rather than losing the customer entirely.

### Medium Risk (Churn Probability 30% - 70%)
*   **Time to Action**: Within 5-7 business days.
*   **Action Owner**: Customer Support / CSM team.
*   **Retention Strategies**:
    *   **Feature Activation Support**: Identify which product features are under-utilized and offer a targeted, hands-on training session.
    *   **Loyalty Upgrades**: Offer premium add-on services or loyalty credits (e.g., higher API limits, free extra licenses) at no additional cost for 3-6 months.
    *   **Contract Optimization**: Transition month-to-month contracts to 1-year plans with a modest discount (e.g., 10-15%).

### Low Risk (Churn Probability < 30%)
*   **Time to Action**: Quarterly.
*   **Action Owner**: Automated engagement / Customer Marketing.
*   **Retention Strategies**:
    *   **NPS Surveys**: Capture positive sentiment and solicit feedback.
    *   **Referral Incentives**: Offer bonuses or billing credits if they refer new customers.
    *   **Cross-selling**: Promote complementary product features or higher-tier subscription bundles.

## 2. Playbooks for Specific Customer Profiles

### Month-to-Month Fiber Optic Customers
*   **Context**: Historically, month-to-month fiber optic plan customers represent the highest volume of telecom churn.
*   **Root Cause**: Often price-sensitive, highly vulnerable to aggressive competitor marketing, or frustrated by unexpected bill increases.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   Proactively target month-to-month customers with 1-year price-lock contracts.
    *   Highlight bundle values (e.g., adding streaming or security services at a discounted price).
    *   Establish regular, automated billing reviews.

### High Monthly Charges with Low Technical Support Usage
*   **Context**: Customers paying premium prices who rarely submit support tickets or interact with customer success.
*   **Root Cause**: Low product engagement or technical roadblocks they suffer in silence, leading to a sudden churn event.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   Initiate a "Tech Support Wellness Check" via email or phone.
    *   Provide direct links to documentation, guides, or video tutorials.
    *   Promote free, guided onboarding or training sessions to boost feature usage.
